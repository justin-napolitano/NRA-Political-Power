I started the NRA-Political-Power project to delve into something that I think often gets oversimplified: political influence, particularly as it concerns the National Rifle Association (NRA). There’s a prevailing thought that political power is all about the dollars flowing from campaign contributions and lobbying efforts. But I believe it’s much more nuanced than that. It’s reputation and social perception that truly drive influence. 

## The Purpose Behind the Repo

I wanted to challenge the common narratives surrounding the NRA’s political strength. The ongoing debate around gun control and the NRA's role in it tends to focus heavily on their financial contributions. But what happens when you peel back those layers?

My essay contemplates the various dimensions of political power, drawing from established theoretical frameworks, like those from Dahl. It queries why an organization like the NRA holds such sway and how it leverages factors beyond just dollars to achieve its objectives.
